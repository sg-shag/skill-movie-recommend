# Light / Warm / Positive Tone Criteria

Derived from pattern analysis of user's 9–10 rated films in watched.csv.

## What counts as «light» for THIS user

**Strong positive signals** (heavy correlation with 9–10 ratings):
- Comedies (especially Soviet/Russian: Ryzanov, Gaidai, Menchov)
- Family/adventure animation (Pixar, Norstein, Disney)
- Biographies of inspiring real people (underdog, triumph stories)
- Nature/wildlife documentaries (friendly, family-oriented)
- Warm dramas with positive resolution
- Music shows (concert films like GriSHkovets)

**Works well — lighter-leaning but with depth:**
- Feel-good road-trip movies
- Romantic comedies with genuine warmth
- «Perfect days» style slice-of-life

**Avoid** — even if well-made (user rates them 9 but they're NOT light):
- War films (Avengers of the Motherland, Come and See, Ballad of a Soldier)
- Crime/thriller/noir (Killer is Watching, Leviathan, Duress)
- Heavy psychological dramas (Requiem for a Dream, Elizabeth)
- Tragedies, dark endings, existential despair
- Horror, extreme violence, abuse-focused plots

## How to assess tone
1. Check genres — if any contain «военный», «криминал» (as main genre), «триллер», «ужасы» — likely NOT light.
2. Check plot description (web search if needed) — is the ending hopeful, bittersweet, or tragic?
3. Avoid films about abuse, oppression, tragedy, or despair unless it's a triumph-over-adversity arc.
